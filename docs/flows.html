What this is not

  • 65 members is not Congress, and 22 managers is not the market. Both are the subset that filed comparable reports in this window.
  • Congressional amounts are bands, not figures. Direction and count are the only honest measures, so no dollar total is shown for that half.
  • Institutional figures are share-count changes priced at the later quarter, so a position that merely appreciated does not appear as a purchase.
  • Everything here was late by construction. 13F lands 45 days after the quarter; 546 of 1,800 congressional transactions (30%) were disclosed past the 45-day statutory deadline, the worst by 880 days.
  • Nothing here is advice, and every position was public knowledge before you read it.
